LLM Plinko

Watch a language model pick its next word. Your prompt leaves your device, becomes numbers, falls through every layer, and lands in the token cloud, where the model's options light up and a dice roll picks one. See it as a 3D tower.

Runs a real small language model in your browser and shows its actual numbers. It downloads 269 MB from Hugging Face once (your browser keeps a copy) and uses about 900 MB of memory, so a laptop or desktop works best.
