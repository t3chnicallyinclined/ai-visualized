Watch an AI pick one word.

A real language model runs in your browser. It takes the start of a sentence, and in a few seconds of arithmetic chooses the next word. This show slows that down and draws every step from the model's own numbers: nothing here is staged.

1 · Choose a model

All four run the loop you are about to watch. They differ in size (layers, numbers per token, dictionary), in what they read, and in a few design choices (how word order gets in, whether the output table is the input table again). The chat model reads a hidden instruction and your message as a chat, then answers instead of continuing. Mamba, the design with no attention, is in the Explorer.

That is all it ever does.

Tokens, spots in the cloud, listening back, layers of fixed knobs, a wall of lights, a weighted dice. One token at a time, from the same frozen numbers. Every frame you saw was drawn from them.

Every language model runs this same loop. The big ones just have more of everything: more layers, far more knobs, a bigger dictionary, and years of reading instead of children's stories. The step is the same; the scale is not.

Want the numbers themselves? The step-by-step walk-through shows every one; the Explorer shows the whole machine at once.
